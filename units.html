Reading the notation: a Cosmossi measurement is written whole.fraction by default, where the number after the point is how many of the 24 parts of the next whole unit you have — not a decimal. 324.12Mre is 324 whole Mereta plus 12⁄24 of a 325th. A bare whole number like 324Mre means exactly that, no remainder. When one level of 1⁄24 isn't precise enough, the notation extends with further .segments, each one 24ths of what's left over — the same way a clock reads 8:40 as the everyday form but can extend to 8:40.21.29 when finer precision is actually needed. So 1.23.12.5Mre is 1 whole, plus 23⁄24, plus 12⁄24 of that 24th, plus 5⁄24 of that. By default, results below are rounded to a single everyday whole.fraction level — check Show full precision under either results panel to see the full nested chain instead. Separately, length also has a smaller named unit for everyday speech, the Sadelle (Sdr): 24 Sadelle make one Delle, the same way 24 Delle make one Roçenna.
