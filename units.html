Reading the notation: a fractional Cosmossi measurement is written whole.fraction, where the number after the point is how many of the 24 parts of the next unit you have — not a decimal. 324.12Mre is 324 whole Mereta plus 12⁄24 of a 325th. 323.23Mre is one twenty-fourth short of a full 324Mre. A bare whole number like 324Mre means exactly that, no remainder. When a measurement needs to be finer than one twenty-fourth of a whole unit, it drops to the next named sub-unit rather than stacking fractions — for length, that sub-unit is the Sadelle (Sdr), 24 of which make one Delle.
